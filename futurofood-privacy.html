Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · Effective: October 15, 2026

Short version: We collect your email to send you our newsletter. We don't sell your data. We don't track you across the web. You can unsubscribe anytime with one click. That's it.

1. Who We Are

FuturoFood ("we", "us", "our") is a digital media publication covering food innovation in Latin America, operated from Alajuela, Costa Rica. Our website is futurofood.com. You can contact us at privacy@futurofood.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter, together with the page you subscribed from and, if you arrived through a shared or campaign link, its campaign tags (utm)
  • Sponsorship inquiries — name, company, email, website, format, budget and message, if you use the form on our Advertise page
  • Messages — if you contact us by email

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, referring URL, approximate location and device type, measured with Google Analytics 4 (see the Cookies section)
  • Email engagement — opens and clicks on newsletters (via Beehiiv)
  • IP address — processed by our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) for security and to determine your approximate country, which decides whether we show the cookie notice; not used for profiling

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Send you the FuturoFood weekly newsletter (the only reason we ask for your email)
  • Send occasional announcements about FuturoFood (new features, special editions)
  • Improve our content based on aggregate, anonymized engagement data
  • Respond to your inquiries and sponsorship requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising targeting, profiling, or selling to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent — when you subscribe to our newsletter, you consent to receiving it. You can withdraw this consent at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Legitimate interests — for analytics and security purposes, where these don't override your rights.

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with the following service providers, who are contractually bound to protect it:

  • Beehiiv — our newsletter, email delivery, and subscriber management platform. Beehiiv Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — website hosting (Cloudflare Pages), CDN and DNS; forwarding of email sent to our @futurofood.com addresses (Email Routing) and delivery of sponsorship form messages (Email Sending). Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google LLC — website analytics (Google Analytics 4), subject to your cookie choice where consent is required (see Cookies); the site's typefaces, loaded from Google Fonts servers, which therefore receive your IP address; and our team's inbox (Gmail), where emails and sponsorship form messages arrive. Google Privacy Policy

External links and content

Our WhatsApp Channel is an external link: when you open it, WhatsApp (Meta) handles your data under its own privacy policy. The share buttons for WhatsApp, X and LinkedIn are plain links too: nothing is sent to those services until you click. Some articles may include YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode) or Vimeo videos, which load from those services and follow their own policies.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you are subscribed to our newsletter. If you unsubscribe, your data is deleted from our active lists within 30 days. Backups may retain data for up to 90 days after deletion.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain types of processing
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@futurofood.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies. We do not use advertising cookies. We use:

  • Essential storage — we don't use cookies for this. Your browser's local and session storage remember your language choice, whether you subscribed, the stories you saved, your cookie choice, the notices you closed and the campaign tags of the link you arrived from (sent with your subscription). This information stays in your browser.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4 sets the _ga and _ga_* cookies to measure visits and how pages are used (for example, reading time and traffic source). Google does not receive your email address. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

9. Children's Privacy

FuturoFood is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. International Transfers

FuturoFood is operated from Costa Rica. Our service providers (Cloudflare, Beehiiv and Google) may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data is transferred from the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent).

11. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including HTTPS encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify newsletter subscribers of material changes.
